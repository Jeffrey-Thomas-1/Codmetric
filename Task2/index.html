Classic Pancakes

Prep: 10 min • Cook: 15 min • Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 3½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1¼ cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter

Directions

  1. Whisk together dry ingredients.
  2. Add milk, egg and melted butter; mix until combined.
  3. Cook 1/4 cup batter per pancake on a greased skillet until golden.

Spaghetti Carbonara

Prep: 10 min • Cook: 15 min • Serves: 2-3

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g pancetta or bacon
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook pasta until al dente.
  2. Fry pancetta until crisp.
  3. Toss hot pasta with beaten eggs and cheese off heat; add pancetta and pepper.

Vegetable Stir-Fry

Prep: 15 min • Cook: 10 min • Serves: 3-4

Ingredients

  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a wok and stir-fry vegetables over high heat.
  2. Add soy sauce and toss until vegetables are tender-crisp.
