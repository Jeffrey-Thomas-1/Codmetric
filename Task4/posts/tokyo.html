Street Restaurents

Tokyo's night markets and izakayas are where the city's flavors come alive. Meandering through small alleys you can try yakitori, ramen, and seasonal street snacks.

Tip: explore neighborhoods like Shinjuku Omoide Yokocho or Golden Gai for an authentic atmosphere. Be mindful of local customs when sharing small tables.
