Cherry Blossom

Mount Fuji is best experienced in the early hours when reflections form on nearby lakes and the summit sits clear against the sky. There are multiple viewpoints across the region that offer varying perspectives.

Tip: check weather forecasts and plan sunrise viewing from spots like Lake Kawaguchi. Consider combining with a local onsen visit for a relaxing end to the day.
