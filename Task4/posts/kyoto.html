Peaceful walks

Wandering peaceful temple gardens before the day crowds arrive is one of the simplest joys of visiting Kyoto. The soft light and quiet paths reveal mossy stone lanterns and hidden alcoves.

Practical tips: arrive early (before 7am) to avoid crowds, respect temple rules, and bring comfortable shoes for uneven paths. Don't miss the smaller sub-temples tucked away from the main routes.
